# The price of AI Slop in 8-bit videogames

I've followed the retrocomputing scene since I got my first Internet connection
in 1999, but I've suddenly lost the interest on it after the
avalanche of automatic decompilations
of classic videogames, unsolicited ports across systems that nobody asked for,
and massive amount of soul-less AI-coded videogames. This blog post shows
a tiny experiment to answer the question that came to my mind: is AI really
so good now at programming Z80 assembly for ancient systems with small
public codebases to get trained with?
